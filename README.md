## Introduction

My wife and I have joked around about Safety Cat,
an imaginary character who gives safety advice.
Sometimes we imagine it giving overly-cautious advice,
other times insufficient.

The initial two drawings are from ChatGPT and are very simple.

My wife might end up drawing something completely different.
If so, those images will almost certainly live somewhere else
since these images are released to the public domain (CC0).

## Prompts

The first drawing of the cat was made by this prompt:
```
I would like you to draw a picture of a cat. It should be a cartoon-like line drawing. The cat should be roughly humanoid in terms of its limbs. But there is no detail of texture or features. Just round the limbs. It should have a tail. The head should be very circular, with very triangular ears. The eyes should be similar to simple Anime eyes. I think they sometimes have a triangular feature. It should have whiskers, fangs, but an oval mouth. Think: "Felix the Cat" but do not copy that.
```

That generated `cat.png`.

I then refined it with this prompt:
```
That is very good. Please add a small mortarboard with tassel to its head between its ears. Have its mouth open in an oval, as if it is talking. Add a free-standing black board in the background. Have the cat raising one hand, with a pointer stick, pointing at the black board. At the top of the black board, have written "Safety Cat Says".
```

That generated `Safety_Cat.png`.

I was impressed with how well the final image matched my
own "mind's eye" picture of what I was going for.
In particular, the facial experssion has just the right
balance of seriousness without severeness.
